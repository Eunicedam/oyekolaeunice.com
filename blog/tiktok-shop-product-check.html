Can your products sell on TikTok Shop? Check these 5 things first

Before you set up a TikTok Shop, check each product one by one. Here is the list I run for every client.

A rejected product or a suspended shop costs you more than time. It can hold back your whole account. So before setup, I go through your catalogue product by product. Not in groups, and not by guessing from the category name.

1. Is the category allowed in your market?

TikTok Shop keeps lists of prohibited and restricted categories, and they differ by country. Some categories are open to everyone, some need approval first, and some cannot be sold at all. Check your target market's current seller policy, because these rules change.

2. Does the product need extra documents?

Restricted categories, like many beauty, supplement and baby products, often need proof before approval: safety certificates, test reports, or brand authorisation if you sell a brand you don't own. Gather these before you apply, not after a rejection.

3. Do the claims on your listing break the rules?

Words like "cures", "guaranteed results" or before-and-after promises can get a listing rejected even when the product itself is allowed. Read your titles, descriptions and images the way a strict reviewer would.

4. Can you ship it the way TikTok expects?

TikTok Shop sets standards for dispatch times and delivery. If a product ships slowly or from far away, late orders can hurt your shop's performance score. Check your stock, your warehouse location and your carrier before you list.

5. Are your product images and details accurate?

Listings with blurry images, missing sizes or details that don't match the product are a common reason for rejection and returns. Use clear photos, correct variants and honest descriptions.

What to do with the results

  • Allowed: list it and plan your first ads.
  • Needs approval: collect the documents first, then apply.
  • Not allowed: leave it out of TikTok Shop and sell it through Meta Ads or Google Shopping instead.
