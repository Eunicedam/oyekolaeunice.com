Questions.

Something else? Message me.

Do you work with local businesses, or only online stores?

Both. For online stores I focus on sales. For local businesses like restaurants, salons, clinics and real estate, I run campaigns that bring calls, WhatsApp messages and bookings from people near you.

What do I get in the free audit?

A review of your store or business page, your offer and your ad setup, with a short list of what to fix first. It is yours to keep whether or not we work together.

Is ad spend included in your packages?

No. Ad spend is paid straight to Meta, TikTok or Google from your own account. My fee covers the setup, management, creatives and reporting.

How soon will I see results?

It depends on your product, offer and budget, so I won't promise a date. As one real example, the pet store on this page made its first sales on day 3 after launch.

Can my products be sold on TikTok Shop?

Not every product or country is eligible. I check your products one by one before setup, so you know what can be listed and what needs changes.

What is Google Merchant Center?

It is Google's tool for getting your products onto Google Shopping, both free listings and Shopping ads. I set it up, connect your product feed and fix any products Google rejects.

Do I keep ownership of my accounts?

Always. Your ad accounts, Pixel, shop and data stay in your name. I work through partner access, which you can remove at any time.
